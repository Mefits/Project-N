# N — mobility and hand signs / experiment 02

Open project.godot in Godot 4.4.1 and press F5. This experiment branch sets the sandbox as its default scene. Alternatively from the project directory: `godot --path . res://lab/mobility_signs/mobility_signs.tscn`. Normal game and animation preview files are unchanged. The isolated sandbox uses a 60 Hz logical update on top of the existing 120 Hz physics setting.

A/D move, Space jump/double jump. F4 cycles five presets (sign modifier / dash): Shift/RMB (default), RMB/Shift, Shift/double Space, RMB/double Space, Tab/Shift (original). In double Space modes the first press jumps immediately; the second within 260 ms requests a dash to the cursor, a later press requests a double jump. Hold the seal modifier and enter WDS (Bolt), SWDW (Orb), ADW (Rush). Re-press the modifier to start a new sequence. Release the modifier to restore directional control, left click to cast within 8 seconds, Escape to cancel (RMB also cancels in presets 3 and 5). F2 compares coast (default) (ground deceleration, gentle air drag) and latch (retain direction sampled on entering signs). F3 toggles moving target; R reset. Aim, jumps and dashes are available during preparation. No invulnerability, two air dashes per landing, double jump, no ordinary melee or hitstun. Rush is an experimental technique with its own dash. Placeholder signs use letters; not canonical. Camera, timing and controls are tentative. No integration into Lab or main arena, no final animation claims.

Test: run then enter seals; jump then enter seals; dash then enter seals; prepare then aim at moving target; compare F2 modes; cancel and expire. Note accidental inputs, ability to evade, comfort with Tab/Shift and mouse, and which direction control is preferable. No real opponent attacks yet, so interruption balance is deferred.
